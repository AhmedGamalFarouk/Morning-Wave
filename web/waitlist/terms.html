What Morning Wave is

A daily good morning between a parent and their grown children. The parent taps once each morning; if they don’t, the family is told gently. It helps families stay in touch.

Morning Wave is not an emergency, medical or safety alert service, and it can’t promise that every good morning or every message arrives on time. Phones run out of battery, lose signal or silence notifications. Never rely on it in an emergency: call 911 or your local emergency number.

Your account

Grown children sign in with Google and must be 18 or older. Parents join with a code from their family and need no account of their own. Keep your family’s codes within your family. You can delete your account at any time, as the privacy policy explains.
