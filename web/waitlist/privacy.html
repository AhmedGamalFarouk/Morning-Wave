Privacy policy

Placeholder for the waitlist only. The full policy and terms for the app go here before launch (plan phase 5).

What we collect on this site

When you join the waitlist we store your email address, which kind of phone you and your parent use, and, if you arrived from a link that names it, the community or video that sent you.

Why

To email you when Morning Wave is ready, and to see how many families use Android, since the first version is Android only.

Who can see it

Only the Morning Wave team. Signups are stored in a database hosted by Supabase. We don’t sell or share your details, and there are no ads or tracking scripts on this site. The page’s fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address like any website you visit.

Removing your details

Email CONTACT-EMAIL and we’ll delete your signup.

Not an emergency service

Morning Wave helps families stay in touch. It is not an emergency or medical alert service. In an emergency, call 911.
