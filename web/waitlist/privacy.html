Morning Wave

Privacy policy

Morning Wave is a daily good morning between a parent and their grown children. This policy covers the Android app and this website.

What the app stores

  • Grown children sign in with Google. We keep your first name and email address from Google.
  • Parents never make an account or give an email. Their phone gets an anonymous sign-in, and we keep the name their family calls them, such as “Mom”.
  • Family mornings: when the parent says good morning, the family’s time zone, the usual morning time, and any days the parent says they’ll be away.
  • Photos and voice notes a child sends. Only the newest of each is kept; sending a new one removes the old.
  • Push notification tokens, so each phone can get its family’s notes and reminders.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): what went wrong, the phone model and Android version. No names, photos or messages.
  • The family plan: RevenueCat records whether you have a subscription, tied to a random ID. Google Play handles the payment; we never see card details.

Why

Only to run Morning Wave: to show the family the parent’s good morning, to let a child know gently when a morning stays quiet, by push notification and email, and to deliver photos and voice notes. We don’t sell or share your details, there are no ads, and nothing is used for tracking.

Who handles it

Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), Google Firebase (push notifications and crash reports), Brevo (the emails the grown children get about a quiet morning, with the parent’s name), RevenueCat and Google Play (the family plan). Each only gets what it needs for that job. Only your own family can see your family’s mornings, photos and voice notes.

Deleting your account

In the app, a grown child taps Delete my account at the bottom of their home screen. That deletes your account, the photos and voice notes you sent, and your push token straight away. If you were the last grown child in a family, the whole family goes too, including the parent’s mornings. If you pay for the family plan, cancel it in Google Play as well. Crash reports expire on their own after 90 days.

A parent, or anyone who can’t open the app, can email myfakemail@atomicmail.io from the address they signed in with, or with the family’s name, and we’ll delete it all within 30 days.

The waitlist

When you join the waitlist on this site we store your email address, which kind of phone you and your parent use, and, if a link brought you here, the community or video that sent you. We use it only to email you when Morning Wave is ready. Email us to remove it. The site’s fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address like any website you visit.

Children

Morning Wave is for adults and their parents. Grown children who sign in must be 18 or older, and it isn’t meant for anyone under 13.

Not an emergency service

Morning Wave helps families stay in touch. It is not an emergency or medical alert service. In an emergency, call 911.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we’ll update this page and the date below. Questions: myfakemail@atomicmail.io.
