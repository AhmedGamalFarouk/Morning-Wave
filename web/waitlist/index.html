Morning Wave

A good-morning from Mom, every day.

A daily check-in for parents who live alone. Each morning your parent taps one big sun button, and the whole family sees they’re up and well. If the morning goes quiet, you hear about it.

How it works

  1. One tap for your parent. No passwords, no menus. A sun button they press when they start their day.
  2. A warm hello for you. “Mom said good morning at 8:14.” Send a heart or a photo back.
  3. A nudge if it goes quiet. If there’s no check-in by the time you choose, we remind your parent first, then let you and a second contact know.
